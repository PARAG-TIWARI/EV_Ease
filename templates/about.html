About EVEase

EVEase is a smart EV charging station locator and slot booking platform designed to solve one of the biggest problems in electric mobility — finding reliable charging stations with real-time availability.

Our Mission

⚡ Smart Charging

Enable EV users to discover nearby charging stations, monitor live availability, and charge without waiting.

📍 Intelligent Navigation

Provide seamless route guidance and location tracking to ensure hassle-free charging experiences.

🌍 Sustainable Future

Support India's EV adoption journey and contribute towards a greener and smarter transportation ecosystem.

Why EVEase?

As electric vehicle adoption increases in India, drivers face problems such as long waiting times, difficulty locating nearby stations, and uncertainty in charger availability. EVEase solves these issues by combining real-time station discovery, smart slot booking, navigation support, and seamless charging management — aligning with the Vision 2047 goal for a smarter and sustainable India.
